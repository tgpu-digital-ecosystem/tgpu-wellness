TGPU Health & Wellbeing

Small daily habits for healthier routines.

TGPU Wellness is a benefit-first wellbeing companion for children, students and families. It supports healthier daily routines through simple guidance, habit support and practical education — without presenting itself as a medical, diagnostic or weight-loss application.

Youth-safe by design

Wellbeing without unhealthy pressure.

The child and student experience is education-first and non-diagnostic.

No weight-loss focus

No calorie counting, weight-loss targets, BMI scores or body-shape goals in the child/student experience.

Education, not diagnosis

Health content stays educational, with clear referral to qualified professionals when appropriate.

Simple by design

Large, age-appropriate controls and low-friction daily routines support accessible everyday use.

Health information: TGPU Wellness provides general educational wellbeing information and is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.
Initial focus

Students first.

The first direction is a lightweight student wellness companion supporting wellbeing alongside learning, family life and community participation.

Future direction

Families, schools & communities.

Parent visibility and school wellbeing challenges can follow after the core student experience is validated.

Language & access

Built for Malaysia, ready to localise.

Malay and English come first, with Arabic localisation architecture for future GCC pilots. Selected routines can also be designed for offline or low-connectivity use where practical.

Future sustainability: A later adult/parent-facing wellness marketplace or brand-partnership layer may be explored for suitable wellness products and services. Any commercial layer should remain clearly separated from children’s core wellbeing experience and follow applicable health, advertising, product and claims requirements.
